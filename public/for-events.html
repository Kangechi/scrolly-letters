Scrolly Letters · Event Letters

Most event emails get skipped.
This one gets scrolled to the end.

A Scrolly Letter turns an invite or recap into a short, cinematic scroll — one line at a time — that people actually finish. Then it sends a signal back to you.

Scroll to see how it fits your platform

01 · Fits into what you already run

Nothing new for your members to learn. A link, not an app.

Every letter is just a shareable URL. It opens full-screen in any browser — no install, no login for the recipient. There are two ways to plug it in, depending on who you are.

For event organizers

Drop-in link

Pick an event, fill in a few lines, get a link. Paste it into your email blast, WhatsApp, or ticket confirmation. Live in minutes, zero engineering.

SACCOs · communities · churches · HR teams · hotels
For platforms

Embed & API

Your members hit “Invite” inside your product; you call our endpoint with the event + recipient and get back a letter link to send. It carries your branding and colors.

ticketing · events · membership · community apps
Your signed-up member→ generates a letter→ recipient opens the link→ RSVP + feedback back to you

02 · How a member sends one

Someone who already signed up brings a friend.

This is the part that spreads. A member who’s attending doesn’t just forward a flyer — they send a personal letter. Here’s the whole path, start to finish.

1

Amara is signed up for the event on your platform.

She sees an “Invite a friend” button next to her ticket or RSVP.

2

She picks the event and adds one personal line.

“You have to be in the room for this one.” The rest of the letter is already built from the event details — she isn’t writing an essay.

3

She sends the link to a friend.

WhatsApp, DM, wherever. It arrives as “Amara invited you,” not as marketing.

4

The friend scrolls through the letter — and RSVPs.

They finish the story, tap “Get your invite,” and can leave a question for the host. That’s exactly the demo below.

5

You get a new attendee and a signal.

The RSVP and any feedback land back with the host — so you know who’s coming and what they want before the day.

03 · Why it’s worth adding

It does three jobs a flyer can’t.

Not a nicer poster — a different mechanic. It gets finished, it travels person-to-person, and it feeds you data.

📖

People finish it

One line at a time is a format built for the phone. Completion beats a wall of text nobody scrolls past the first paragraph of.

🔗

It spreads member-to-member

A personal invite from a friend converts far better than a broadcast. Every attendee becomes a warm channel to the next.

📡

It sends a signal back

RSVPs and questions flow to the host. You learn who’s coming and what would make them show up — before the event, not after.

🎨

It carries your brand

Colors, host name, and tone are all yours. To your members it feels like a feature of your platform, not a third-party bolt-on.
